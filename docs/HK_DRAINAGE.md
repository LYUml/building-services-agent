# Hong Kong building drainage: source research

The demand tools currently calculate scoped quantities; they do not establish a compliant sanitary network. Keep Buildings Department aboveground building drainage requirements distinct from Drainage Services Department public sewer design and from WSD potable-water requirements.

The official [PNAP APP-164](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/practice-notes-and-circular-letters/pnap/APP/APP164.pdf), retrieved2026-11-11 with printed November2023 revision, is pinned in [source evidence](evidence/hk-drainage-source.json). Paragraph2(d)/(e) distinguishes buildings over3 through20 storeys from buildings over20 for separate pipework at the stack/manhole discharge level. The latter also addresses the floor above. Footnote5 distinguishes total building height from storeys served by a particular stack, with special podium/basement scope.

The classroom's ground-floor exclusion is not a universal all-height rule. Determine the actual building scope, discharge level, connected floors and fixtures before DU aggregation. Paragraphs3/4 and the appendices require separate works-type/application review; current document availability alone does not establish a project's applicable edition.

`bsa.drainage_scope.separate_discharge_floors` now provides a tested planning helper for 2(d)/(e). It returns relative floor offsets from the stack's manhole-discharge floor: [0] for more than3 through20 storeys and [0,1] above20. The discharge floor is not assumed to be ground level. Supply total building storeys, or an explicitly selected podium-only/basement-only scope and its count; stack served-floor count is not a substitute. Counts at or below3 remain outside the supported height bands, without implying exemption from other requirements.

Edition, works-scope and survey evidence are mandatory nonempty reviewed references. Unknown scope/count/evidence yields pending and no floor selection. The helper does not fetch or authenticate those references, decide legal applicability, verify fixture inventories, select actual floor labels or inspect pipe routes. Its tests cover 3/4/20/21 boundaries, special scopes, missing evidence and invalid counts. No new dependency or rule engine was introduced.

No APP-164 executable conformity rule pack, saved-model binding or full sanitary design is claimed. This helper is not yet connected to the generic regulations workflow or DU inventory. Cross-ventilation, offsets/restricted zones, traps, roof outlets, pipe capacity, current Drainage Regulations and other applicable requirements remain unresolved. The official PDF stays in ignored runs; source bytes and exact references are recorded without republishing a course document.
