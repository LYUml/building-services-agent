# Rectangular air-tree pressure budgets

## Selected dimensions regenerate the source model

Workflow kind rectangular_model_design accepts project/model/binding/catalogue/geometry and optional requires_pass. Standalone: `python -m bsa.rectangular_model_design <geometry-free-project> --model <source-model> --binding <binding> --catalogue <catalogue> --geometry <wall-options> --out <new-run>`. Source model project_uuid is required. The geometry contract has basis `rectangular walls; fixed centrelines and endpoints`, sources, and exactly one positive sourced wall_thickness_m per catalogue option. Width/height and roughness remain catalogue inputs; wall thickness is not inferred from duct size.

The adapter reuses existing finite search, source-bound author/reconcile and saved-IFC identity comparison. It filters source-space-incompatible outer walls while retaining reasons, preserves baseline model/search failures, saves the selected source model and geometry-free skeleton, reauthors/reopens native IFC and repeats bound pressure analysis. Original route endpoint world positions and all semantic object/port GUIDs are independently checked against the baseline. The new bound required pressure must match the selected numerical candidate. Original sources remain unchanged. No-feasible or no-fitting option returns failure without a selected model. Outer-space containment is not clash/clearance or installation acceptance.

Actual runs/rectangular-model-design-workflow-v1 expands the synthetic single 100 x 100 mm straight duct to selected 500 x 300 mm internal dimensions with separately assumed 1 mm walls. The two fixed endpoint world coordinates and IFC identities are preserved; saved bound pressure is 37.3702403 Pa and the final engineering review stays pending. The first native trial exposed NumPy scalar coordinates rejected by the strict source validator; conversion to ordinary numeric coordinates corrected the implementation without weakening validation, and the failed run is preserved. Four new tests cover native selected rerun, source/endpoint preservation, space rejection and no-feasible workflow gates. See evidence/rectangular-air/model-design.json. This supersedes earlier automatic-revision-pending notes for supported straight chains only; arbitrary routing, finite bends/junction physics, real products, fan/plot/regulatory acceptance remain pending.

## Source-model bound pressure workflow

`python -m bsa.rectangular_binding <geometry-free-project.json> --model <source-model.json> --binding <binding.json> --out <new-run>` composes the existing port-chain binding, BIM author, independent native rectangular-wall reader and pressure engine. Workflow kind rectangular_network_binding accepts project/model/binding and optional requires_pass. Its source files and seven adapter modules enter workflow input inventory. See examples/rectangular-air/binding-plan.json for a one-invocation synthetic case.

Omit network node elevation_m and segment length_m/width_m/height_m/diameter_m. Exact node/segment maps associate node IDs to device.port references and each segment to an ordered, unique device chain. Every source-model duct must be covered. Only explicit straight hollow rectangular ducts may form these routes; mixed dimensions require separate numerical segments. Source port distances and internal dimensions produce a new calculation input; saved IFC profile/placement/metadata/native wall geometry are independently checked and saved directed ports/connections reconciled against the derived input before fresh pressure analysis. The original port-chain node modes and total-pressure/local-K contracts still apply. This does not infer junction geometry or branch losses from coincident ports.

Actual runs/rectangular-bound-workflow-v1 verifies a synthetic single straight 2 m, 500 x 300 mm internal duct and passes the numerical task; full review remains pending. It neither represents a complete fan/terminal assembly nor establishes whole-building coverage. Four new tests exercise actual saved geometry/pressure, duplicate geometry rejection, saved profile mutation before analysis, positive and insufficient-pressure workflow gates. Five circular-binding and four rectangular-geometry regression tests pass. Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/binding.json. Selected-catalogue automatic model revision, finite bends/transitions/junctions, native plotted drawings and full physical/regulatory acceptance remain pending.

## Explicit straight wall geometry

The existing bsa.bim author now accepts geometry_scope `axis-aligned envelopes and explicit rectangular duct walls` and body_shape `straight rectangular duct wall` on IfcDuctSegment. See examples/rectangular-air/straight-wall.json. Supply independently sourced InternalWidth_m, InternalHeight_m and WallThickness_m, the matching outer envelope, two opposite end-face centre ports and ordinary source/space/system records. Width/height refer to the local profile axes determined by the positive-axis frame; do not swap them blindly when changing route axis. X/Y/Z axis-aligned straight walls are supported; arbitrary rotation, bends, transitions and junction fabrication are not.

It reuses pinned IfcOpenShell 0.8.5 standard IfcRectangleHollowProfileDef/IfcExtrudedAreaSolid, the existing metre/stable-GUID/system/port author and native reopen, circular-wall closed two-manifold/through-bore checks and Manifold connected-solid/mass verification. Sharp corners only: fillets and profile offsets reject. Saved profile dimensions, metadata, depth, object/solid placements, native bounds/centroid/volume and outward port axes are checked separately. No mesh repair or widened tolerances is used.

Actual runs/rectangular-wall-native-v2 authors and reopens a synthetic 500 x 300 mm internal, 1 mm uniform wall, 2 m straight duct. Wall volume is 0.003208 m3; through-bore Euler characteristic is zero and the native mesh is one connected closed wall. Four new native tests exercise three axis directions and saved profile/fillet/position/metadata mutations. This is not the selected network's bound geometry, sheet-metal fabrication detail, insulation, a native DWG drawing or completed duct installation. Those remain pending. Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/straight-wall.json.

## Verified selected-input handoff

A `rectangular_network` task may now use depends_on on an earlier rectangular_network_design instead of project. Replacement project input is rejected. Failed/no-feasible producers leave the consumer unexecuted. The executor pins producer artifact bytes in memory; selected_input additionally checks source identities, complete required artifact hashes, reconstructs the ranked selected candidate from the original project/catalogue and matches the saved network and selected numerical input hash. The consumer then executes fresh pressure analysis on that saved selected file. These checks establish sourced candidate correspondence, not document authenticity or geometry/code acceptance.

Use examples/rectangular-air/selected-plan.json. Actual runs/rectangular-selected-handoff-v1 passes sizing and inherited analysis while manual review stays pending. The consumer input hash matches air/selected-network.json and differs from undersized.json. Changed selection is rejected even if its artifact hash is rewritten; replacement and no-feasible cases are retained in tests. Nineteen rectangular tests plus seven dependency-diagnosis tests pass. Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/selected-handoff.json. This supersedes earlier standalone-project-only notes for the rectangular pressure task; it does not add IFC geometry inheritance.

## Finite catalogue sizing

Use `.venv/Scripts/python -m bsa.rectangular_design examples/rectangular-air/undersized.json --catalogue examples/rectangular-air/catalogue.json --out runs/new-rectangular-sizing`, or workflow kind `rectangular_network_design` with project/catalogue and optional requires_pass. The adapter reuses the existing exhaustive circular-tree search, ranking, baseline retention, candidate report and saved selected-project rerun. It supplies rectangular dimension validation and the actual-area pressure calculator; no second optimizer is introduced. Its selected-network.json remains geometry-free rectangular input, not an IFC model or an input to circular sizing/model-binding consumers.

Catalogue fields follow network_design: exact sourced options/allowed_options coverage, explicit hydraulic_basis, objective and max_evaluations (up to 10000). Options provide width_m and height_m instead of diameter_m, plus roughness_m, material_score_per_m and source. Score is a supplied metric, not inferred cost or material quantity. The explicit fixed-route/local-K assumption must be justified across every allowed size; real fittings can change with width/height, aspect ratio and velocity reference. Method applicability remains separately sourced. Preserve all rejected candidates; no-feasible fails workflow and creates no selected project. Declared bound overflow rejects rather than truncate. Selected dimensions are saved and freshly reanalysed before success.

Actual `runs/rectangular-sizing-v1` starts from an undersized 100 x 100 mm synthetic tree and evaluates 27 supplied combinations. MAIN selects 500 x 300 mm, BR-A/BR-B select 300 x 200 mm, at supplied length-weighted score 54. The MAIN result also follows an independent velocity bound: its 0.5 m3/s and 7 m/s target require at least 0.0714286 m2, so the 0.06 m2 medium option cannot pass. Baseline failures are retained and selected rerun passes; engineering review remains pending. Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/sizing.json. Six sizing plus 22 related pressure/circular-sizing tests pass. These are synthetic dimension choices, not manufacturer, spatial-fit, fan or regulatory acceptance.

Use `.venv/Scripts/python -m bsa.rectangular_network examples/rectangular-air/project.json --out runs/new-rectangular-air` or workflow kind `rectangular_network` with `project` and optional earlier `requires_pass`. The workflow inventories the project and both calculation modules; it accepts no IFC inheritance or replacement geometry contract. Outputs are network.json, segments.csv, a conditional HTML report and a byte manifest. This calculates prescribed flows, not a passive branch-flow or fan-curve operating point.

The explicit model is `steady incompressible rectangular air tree`. It reuses the existing circular tree's topology validation, withdrawal accumulation, total-pressure accounting, target checks and output writer, with a separate geometry adapter. Each segment supplies positive internal width_m/height_m instead of diameter_m. Actual velocity is Q/(width*height); hydraulic diameter is 2*width*height/(width+height) for Reynolds number, relative roughness and Darcy-Weisbach length ratio. The upstream pinned fluids 1.3.1 Colebrook solver supplies the Darcy factor. Circular-equivalent area is never substituted. Fitting K uses actual local mean velocity and must have a justified reference section; tee and unequal-area coefficients cannot be copied without conversion/applicability evidence.

Every other input/source/total-pressure contract follows NETWORKS.md. `method_basis` requires a known source plus explicit true constant_density_air, fully_developed, hydraulic_diameter_applicable and level_common_reference assertions. These assertions are not authenticated engineering facts. All node elevations must be equal. Positive-flow Re below 4000 is rejected; the circular 64/Re laminar law is not applied to rectangles. Zero-flow internal branches retain zero losses. Rigid fully filled uniform sections are represented as pressure elements only; no spatial geometry or thermal model is authored.

Method references: [ASHRAE Fundamentals duct design, section 3.1, equations 18/19/24](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F17/SI/f17_ch21/f17_ch21_si.aspx) describes turbulent noncircular hydraulic-diameter correlation and its limits; section 3.2 distinguishes local reference velocity. [fluids friction documentation](https://fluids.readthedocs.io/fluids.friction.html) documents Darcy Colebrook calculation. These are method references, not Hong Kong regulations or freely redistributable fitting tables. No ASHRAE tables or figures are copied. fluids is already pinned, MIT licensed and attributed in the repository's upstream inventory; no new dependency or pressure engine was introduced.

The example is explicitly synthetic: MAIN 0.5 x 0.3 m, actual area 0.15 m2 and hydraulic diameter 0.375 m, carries 0.5 m3/s at 3.333333 m/s. Its Reynolds number is 83333.3333 and upstream Darcy factor 0.0197880072, giving 2.814294 Pa straight friction for the assumed 8 m main. These values do not select a real duct or fan. Actual `runs/rectangular-air-workflow-v1` passes the pressure-budget task while full review stays pending. Evidence is `evidence/rectangular-air/workflow.json`.

Pending: real flow/route/property/roughness evidence, developing-flow and shape-correlation applicability, detailed fittings and system effects, balancing/dampers, fan selection and power, leakage/acoustics, insulation, fire/smoke and full Hong Kong requirements, saved IFC/CAD binding and native plotted drawings. No rule pass or geometry-bound claim is emitted. Supply/terminal inputs are total pressure at a common reference; do not substitute static pressure or use this level-only adapter for stack effects.
