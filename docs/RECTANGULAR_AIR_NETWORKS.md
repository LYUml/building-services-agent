# Rectangular air-tree pressure budgets

## Independent saved-entity audit

The rectangular renderer now calls rectangular_drawing_audit on the actual saved DXF against already source-checked native model records. Workflow repeats this audit on AutoCAD's independently reopened/exported source-export.dxf. It reconstructs exact expected outer rectangles, nonzero duct centreline, inside-size/length and context-envelope notes, positions, layers and GUID coverage without invoking the writer or trusting its success flag. Duct source records carry verified world axis endpoints as well as native bounds/dimensions.

The reader checks metre units, finite planar coordinates, supported LINE/TEXT entities, exact text/style, visibility and plot-enabled layers, and raw group-67 flags before ezdxf can normalize them. Missing/extra/shifted entities, wrong sizes/GUIDs and hidden/nonplotting entities reject. Existing approximate ezdxf text-box/text-graphic guards are reused; these do not certify actual glyphs or arbitrary congested layouts. Expected records must come from the saved-model/source checks, not invented data or an unauthenticated manifest.

Actual runs/rectangular-independent-sheet-v1 passes both saved and native-export independent 11-entity audits, actual DWG reopen and fixed-scale PDF checks. Its actual PDF was rendered and inspected; source size/length and envelope-only notes remain readable. Seven new mutation/audit tests plus five renderer/gate and five text-layout tests pass (17 distinct tests). Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/independent-sheet.json. This supersedes the earlier snapshot-only audit limitation for this supported sheet contract; full source authentication, hidden lines, installation detail and engineering/Hong Kong acceptance remain pending.

## Finite catalogue sizing

Use `.venv/Scripts/python -m bsa.rectangular_design examples/rectangular-air/undersized.json --catalogue examples/rectangular-air/catalogue.json --out runs/new-rectangular-sizing`, or workflow kind `rectangular_network_design` with project/catalogue and optional requires_pass. The adapter reuses the existing exhaustive circular-tree search, ranking, baseline retention, candidate report and saved selected-project rerun. It supplies rectangular dimension validation and the actual-area pressure calculator; no second optimizer is introduced. Its selected-network.json remains geometry-free rectangular input, not an IFC model or an input to circular sizing/model-binding consumers.

Catalogue fields follow network_design: exact sourced options/allowed_options coverage, explicit hydraulic_basis, objective and max_evaluations (up to 10000). Options provide width_m and height_m instead of diameter_m, plus roughness_m, material_score_per_m and source. Score is a supplied metric, not inferred cost or material quantity. The explicit fixed-route/local-K assumption must be justified across every allowed size; real fittings can change with width/height, aspect ratio and velocity reference. Method applicability remains separately sourced. Preserve all rejected candidates; no-feasible fails workflow and creates no selected project. Declared bound overflow rejects rather than truncate. Selected dimensions are saved and freshly reanalysed before success.

Actual `runs/rectangular-sizing-v1` starts from an undersized 100 x 100 mm synthetic tree and evaluates 27 supplied combinations. MAIN selects 500 x 300 mm, BR-A/BR-B select 300 x 200 mm, at supplied length-weighted score 54. The MAIN result also follows an independent velocity bound: its 0.5 m3/s and 7 m/s target require at least 0.0714286 m2, so the 0.06 m2 medium option cannot pass. Baseline failures are retained and selected rerun passes; engineering review remains pending. Evidence: evidence/rectangular-air/sizing.json. Six sizing plus 22 related pressure/circular-sizing tests pass. These are synthetic dimension choices, not manufacturer, spatial-fit, fan or regulatory acceptance.

Use `.venv/Scripts/python -m bsa.rectangular_network examples/rectangular-air/project.json --out runs/new-rectangular-air` or workflow kind `rectangular_network` with `project` and optional earlier `requires_pass`. The workflow inventories the project and both calculation modules; it accepts no IFC inheritance or replacement geometry contract. Outputs are network.json, segments.csv, a conditional HTML report and a byte manifest. This calculates prescribed flows, not a passive branch-flow or fan-curve operating point.

The explicit model is `steady incompressible rectangular air tree`. It reuses the existing circular tree's topology validation, withdrawal accumulation, total-pressure accounting, target checks and output writer, with a separate geometry adapter. Each segment supplies positive internal width_m/height_m instead of diameter_m. Actual velocity is Q/(width*height); hydraulic diameter is 2*width*height/(width+height) for Reynolds number, relative roughness and Darcy-Weisbach length ratio. The upstream pinned fluids 1.3.1 Colebrook solver supplies the Darcy factor. Circular-equivalent area is never substituted. Fitting K uses actual local mean velocity and must have a justified reference section; tee and unequal-area coefficients cannot be copied without conversion/applicability evidence.

Every other input/source/total-pressure contract follows NETWORKS.md. `method_basis` requires a known source plus explicit true constant_density_air, fully_developed, hydraulic_diameter_applicable and level_common_reference assertions. These assertions are not authenticated engineering facts. All node elevations must be equal. Positive-flow Re below 4000 is rejected; the circular 64/Re laminar law is not applied to rectangles. Zero-flow internal branches retain zero losses. Rigid fully filled uniform sections are represented as pressure elements only; no spatial geometry or thermal model is authored.

Method references: [ASHRAE Fundamentals duct design, section 3.1, equations 18/19/24](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F17/SI/f17_ch21/f17_ch21_si.aspx) describes turbulent noncircular hydraulic-diameter correlation and its limits; section 3.2 distinguishes local reference velocity. [fluids friction documentation](https://fluids.readthedocs.io/fluids.friction.html) documents Darcy Colebrook calculation. These are method references, not Hong Kong regulations or freely redistributable fitting tables. No ASHRAE tables or figures are copied. fluids is already pinned, MIT licensed and attributed in the repository's upstream inventory; no new dependency or pressure engine was introduced.

The example is explicitly synthetic: MAIN 0.5 x 0.3 m, actual area 0.15 m2 and hydraulic diameter 0.375 m, carries 0.5 m3/s at 3.333333 m/s. Its Reynolds number is 83333.3333 and upstream Darcy factor 0.0197880072, giving 2.814294 Pa straight friction for the assumed 8 m main. These values do not select a real duct or fan. Actual `runs/rectangular-air-workflow-v1` passes the pressure-budget task while full review stays pending. Evidence is `evidence/rectangular-air/workflow.json`.
