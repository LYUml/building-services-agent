# MEP jurisdiction checks

For fresh EPANET graph or circulation pipe results, use `bsa.epanet_pipe_regulations` as documented in docs/EPANET_PIPE_RULES.md. Require exact per-pipe bases, separately sourced density/fluid applicability and code-diameter classifications. Actual speed and distributed friction are derived; preserve hydraulic failures and regulatory reviews. Use workflow kind epanet_pipe_regulations and requires_pass gates. Chained circulation duty and water graph tasks must use identical hydraulic input bytes. Fluid/thermal assumption consistency and IFC correspondence remain separate review items.

At intake and before delivery, read docs/REGULATORY_COVERAGE.md and run `bsa.regulation_coverage` with the selected jurisdiction catalog, sourced per-topic decisions and optional workflow plan. The Hong Kong catalog regulations/hk/coverage-2026.11.09.json adds HVAC water piping; retain the older snapshot for existing evidence. Resolve every missing applicability decision; retain unimplemented and unscheduled checks in the requirement matrix. A scheduled module is not an executed or complete check. The catalog is a planning aid, not an exhaustive legal determination; exclusions require project evidence and rationale.

Read repository-relative docs/REGULATION_PACKS.md. Establish the project's jurisdiction, building/works scope, relevant dates and required disciplines from the brief and official sources. Select explicit pack IDs and edition decisions; do not choose the latest edition merely from today's date. Distinguish legislation, codes, utility conditions, guidance and contract specifications. Missing statutory/application facts remain pending. The current Hong Kong modules cover selected electrical voltage-drop, office LPD and HVAC load-design condition paths, not full conformity.

Use the pack's official index/publication links to review amendments and transition provisions for the actual project. Fetch the required cached sources with scripts/fetch_regulation_sources.py; a hash mismatch needs source/version review and a new deliberately pinned pack rather than rewriting old evidence. Preserve source attribution and keep original official PDFs in the local ignored cache. A source catalog is not an executable rule set.

Choose the binding matching the available project evidence:

- For air-conditioning water piping, read docs/HK_WATER_PIPING.md and use `bsa.water_pipe_regulations` with the original circular-tree network and exact per-segment bases. Derive velocity and distributed friction through the solver; separately source code-diameter classification and flow/pump-control mode. Preserve hydraulic failures even when scoped rules pass. This is not domestic-water compliance, a loop solver, or installed control verification; Use workflow kind `water_pipe_regulations` and downstream `requires_pass` gates; direct `network` consumers must use identical input bytes. For saved circular IFC routes, use `depends_on` on an earlier `network_coordination` producer and omit `project`; native artifacts and geometry are rechecked before rules. Further CAD/plotting, optimization and changed downstream models still need separate source correspondence.

- For sourced facts only, use `bsa.regulations` or workflow kind `regulations`. Facts and edition decisions require evidence references; their strings do not authenticate the underlying documents.
- For electrical origin-to-equipment voltage drop, read docs/ELECTRICAL_PATHS.md and use `electrical_regulations` with original project, topology, basis, pack and sources. It recalculates supported single-phase radial paths and preserves branch failures. Never substitute a branch-only drop, starting transient or unsupported three-phase calculation. Topology completeness, feeder protection and coefficient applicability remain separate.
- For office lighting, read docs/HK_LIGHTING_RULES.md. Use `lighting_regulations` with shared electrical and geometry inputs plus exact per-space bases. This derives full fixture watts and rectangular source-space areas; it does not verify saved IFC geometry or statutory internal area. Do not reduce LPD with demand/dimming factors, infer driver losses, pool separate rooms or invent exclusions. Non-light classes and unresolved scope remain visible.
- For HVAC design-basis checks read docs/HK_HVAC_RULES.md and docs/HVAC_IDF_RULES.md. Use `bsa.hvac_idf_rules` for supported saved native static sizing controls and design days, with exact zone basis coverage. It derives native numeric values, checks the whole RH band conservatively and includes winter daily-range minima. For an executed design-day workflow use `hvac_regulations` with `depends_on` on its earlier `energy` producer: both native variants and SQL/completion artifact hashes are verified. Add downstream `requires_pass` gates. Standalone use still needs manual run correspondence; arbitrary controls and other energy kinds remain unsupported. Separate JSON facts cannot prove model correspondence. Keep maintained-state performance, load-method, equipment and remaining HVAC clauses pending.
- When lighting performance is required, also use ../mep-lighting-analysis/SKILL.md. LPD and actual IES/Radiance illuminance are distinct checks. scripts/run_hk_lighting_demo.py is a synthetic integration demonstration, not an actual project's target/photometry source.

In a source-linked workflow, declare `requires_pass` for relevant downstream authoring tasks using docs/WORKFLOWS.md. Bind the checks to the same project/geometry as their consumers; known electrical/lighting consumers validate identical project/geometry bytes, including inherited producer inputs. Other cross-domain correspondences and mandatory-check discovery remain manual. Include outstanding statutory/control/domain requirements in the requirement matrix even when no executable rule supports them.

Inspect per-circuit/per-space findings, input/source hashes and coverage, then use `python -m bsa.workflow verify` and `python -m bsa.workflow_status` for recorded integrity and blockers. Preserve `pending`, `review_required`, `not_applicable` and branch failures. An exception to one rule is not whole-project approval. Even a passing supported task leaves `regulatory_compliance: not_established` and complete engineering acceptance unresolved; use ../mep-delivery-check/SKILL.md for the full deliverable review.

For finite water-pipe selection use `bsa.water_pipe_design` as documented in docs/HK_WATER_PIPING.md. Require sourced code-diameter classifications for every catalogue option. Preserve all hydraulic and rule rejections; never choose a merely hydraulic pass. For supported straight-route selected IFC authoring use `bsa.regulated_water_model`: it reopens revised geometry, checks stable identities and repeats hydraulic/rule checks. Clash, products, plotted drawings and full engineering acceptance remain separate.

Workflow kind `regulated_water_model` exposes its selected IFC/network to downstream tasks. Use `ifc_clash` with an explicitly sourced policy, then gate drawing tasks on the clash result. A generated DXF is not native DWG or inspected-plot acceptance. See docs/HK_WATER_PIPING.md for the full task contract.

For a reproducible synthetic combined thermal/HK-pipe workflow, use scripts/run_hk_circulation_demo.py with a new repository-local output directory and the hash-pinned official BEC PDF. Read docs/EPANET_PIPE_RULES.md. It runs positive, regulatory-review and insufficient-duty cases; demonstration success does not mean engineering acceptance.

For thermal and pipe-rule checks on a saved water_binding circulation producer, use bsa.ifc_circulation_checks with its original template/binding, thermal/rule bases and pinned pack/source cache. Read docs/WATER_BINDING.md. It rechecks native artifact hashes and saved geometry, rejects replacement hydraulic input and preserves both check results. Use workflow kind ifc_circulation_checks with depends_on on earlier water_coordination, thermal_basis, rule_basis, pack and sources. Producer geometry/template/binding are inherited; replacement inputs are forbidden. Gate downstream tasks with requires_pass. This does not establish complete compliance or physical fitting/visual acceptance.
