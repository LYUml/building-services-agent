# Synthetic network pressure budgets

Using air.json, aggregate the prescribed terminal flows in the circular air tree. Calculate Darcy friction and local losses, check the specified velocity and total-pressure targets, and identify the critical path. The scenario has no elevation change and does not ask for a fan operating-point solution.

Using water.json, calculate the prescribed 1 L/s flow through the given 40 mm internal diameter pipe including its 20 m route, local K value and 5 m rise. Determine the source total-pressure budget needed to retain 100 kPa total pressure at the outlet. Treat all inputs as explicit synthetic assumptions and do not claim a pump or code-approved design.
