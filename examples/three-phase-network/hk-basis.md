# Synthetic selected-clause basis

This is an original software test fixture, not a building classification, actual equipment rating, protection selection or statutory assessment. For this conditional scenario only, select BEC 2024 and assume the electrical installation lies within its relevant scope.

Assume both named circuits are three-phase four-wire circuits with single-phase loads. Assume the modelled constant-PQ operating point is the normal-service RMS design condition and the sinusoidal fundamental model is applicable to that condition. These are explicit scenario assertions, not independently verified harmonic or physical-neutral evidence.

For the selected-clause test, assume the feeder protective-device rating is 400 A and the branch protective-device rating is 63 A. These classification inputs are not selected hardware or verified cable ratings. They are separate from the network example's 100 A study current target. Other protection, neutral, harmonics, metering and statutory checks remain unresolved.
